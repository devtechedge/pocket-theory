### Key Strategic Takeaways for POCKET THEORY
1. **Breadth with restraint:** Portronics and Ambrane prove that Indian D2C gadget buyers shop across five connected daily contexts - **Power & Charging**, **Audio**, **Wearables & Trackers**, **Desk/Computer/Creator Gear**, and **Automotive & Travel** - often bundling a cable or wall charger with a primary device. POCKET THEORY covers this exact 40-SKU functional footprint under one coherent industrial design language.
2. **Zero fake urgency:** Nearly every incumbent relies on countdown timers, `80%+ off` inflated MRPs, and unverified vanity counters. POCKET THEORY differentiates through **calm, honest pricing** (modest, realistic MRP vs selling-price spreads where applicable, integer-paise GST transparency, and zero countdown timers or fake social-proof popups).
3. **Engineering-grade spec & compatibility tables:** Buyers choosing between a `20W`, `33W`, or `65W GaN` charger - or between a `100W` vs `240W E-Marked` USB-C cable - need clear port matrices, protocol notes, Wh airline limits, and explicit `"Pending supplier verification"` flags where lab test sheets are not yet attached.

## 2. Reputable Market & Category Demand Signals (2025 - Q2 2026)

### Primary Industry Sources Consulted
1. **IDC India Monthly Wearable Device Tracker (Full-Year 2025, published April 2026)**
   - Secondary reporting URLs inspected 11 Nov 2026: <https://www.idc.com/promo/wearablevendor/> and <https://sahha.ai/blog/wearable-market-splitting-in-two-2026/> (citing IDC India CY2025 release of 20 April 2026).
   - **Key signals:** India shipped **114.2 million wearable units in CY2025 (-4.0% YoY)**. Within that total, **earwear accounted for 84.7 million units (+1.4% YoY)** - roughly three-quarters of Indian wearable volume - while **smartwatches declined 17.6% YoY to 28.9 million units** at an overall wearable ASP near **USD 20.30 (~INR 1,700)**.
2. **Counterpoint Research - India TWS & Smartwatch Shipment Trackers (Q2 2026)**
   - URLs inspected 11 Nov 2026:
     - <https://counterpointresearch.com/en/reports/India-tws-shipments-by-model-tracker-Q2-2026>
     - <https://counterpointresearch.com/en/reports/india-smartwatch-shipment-by-model-tracker-q2-2026>
   - **Key signals:** In Q2 2026, India TWS shipments contracted 11% YoY and smartwatch shipments contracted nearly 20% YoY as first-time entry-tier adoption matured into a **replacement-driven upgrade cycle** where buyers scrutinize ANC quality, AMOLED displays, call mic clarity, and build longevity rather than buying disposable ultra-cheap units.
3. **Global & Regional Form-Factor Shifts (IDC Worldwide Wearable Tracker, July 2026 & Omdia Q2 2026)**
   - URL inspected 11 Nov 2026: <https://www.idc.com/promo/wearablevendor/>
   - **Key signals:** Hearables represent ~65% of global wearable shipments (407.6M forecast for 2026), with open-ear wireless stereo (OWS) sport designs and USB-C wired audio regaining share alongside laptop-grade GaN charging (driven by universal USB-C adoption across smartphones, tablets, and ultrabooks).

### Methodology & Limitations
- **Macro market trackers vs store performance:** IDC and Counterpoint figures measure *wholesale/channel shipments across the Indian market*, not POCKET THEORY sales. POCKET THEORY is a newly built single-merchant reference application with **zero live commercial order history**.
- **Category weighting rationale:** Because earwear, USB-C fast charging/power banks, and practical desk/car utilities represent the most durable replacement categories in India while basic smartwatches face replacement-cycle saturation, our 40-SKU seed allocates **12 SKUs to Power & Charging**, **9 SKUs to Audio** (spanning ANC TWS, low-latency gaming, OWS open-ear, neckband, ANC over-ear, USB-C wired, compact speaker, party speaker, desktop soundbar), **11 SKUs to Desk/Computer/Creator utilities**, **5 SKUs to Automotive & Travel**, and a focused **3-SKU Wearables & Tracker line** (1 AMOLED watch, 1 lightweight fitness band, 1 BLE item tracker).
